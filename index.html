The problem

Monitoring is not evidence.

Ratios you can't verify

Performance ratios inherit whatever the sensors say. A mislabeled or drifting sensor rewrites a site's reputation for years, and nothing flags it.

Outages nobody explains

Production stops, and no record says why or who pays. The loss quietly becomes yours.

Records that fail diligence

At refinancing and exit, an unverifiable operating history is priced against you. The caveat costs more than the kilowatt-hours.

Why was Site 07 down on June 14?

Site 07 was offline for 2 hours 45 minutes on June 14, from 14:02 to 16:47 EDT. Every inverter tripped together on grid protection, and none on an equipment fault.

All six inverters went offline in the same polling window E-114, each with a grid-protection code at its first reading and no equipment code E-115. That shows the site lost its grid connection. It does not show whether the utility's equipment or the site's own breaker opened. The revenue meter recorded no export for the whole window E-116.

The monitoring platform logged no downtime that day E-117. Against the irradiance at the time E-118, lost generation is 2.1 MWh, upper bound 2.4 MWh. None of it is booked anywhere yet.

Grid protection · origin open Meter verified Not in site record
What this cannot show Which device opened, the utility's or the site's. The utility's circuit log and the operator's switching record settle that, and the request for both is drafted E-119.
